About Aayush Jaiswal

I’m Aayush Jaiswal, an AI Solutions Engineer at CoreWeave. I help enterprise teams put model training, evaluation, inference, and AI agents into practice, drawing on hands-on ML engineering and generative-model research.

From models to systems

At Trell, I built multilingual video recommendations with TensorFlow Recommenders and ScaNN, alongside data pipelines and production monitoring. At Indiana University, I researched 3D diffusion models for brain imaging and implemented spherical CNNs in TensorFlow.

At Weights & Biases and CoreWeave, my work spans enterprise ML troubleshooting, automated bug reproduction, and agent observability. I collaborated with CrowdStrike on integrating Weave tracing with Falcon AIDR security controls and co-led an inference workshop at Fully Connected 2026.

Sharing the work

I write about diffusion models, recommendation systems, and ML operations. I also teach through technical talks and workshops, including speech-model fine-tuning sessions in Boston and Chicago.
